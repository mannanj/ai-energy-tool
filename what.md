# What

A simple chat bot that helps Fairfax County residents who are Dominion Energy customers understand,
pay, or get help with their electric bill.

We start with one question:

> "The rates for electricity have increased making it hard to make ends meet, what can I do?"

You describe your situation in plain words. The bot asks one screener question at a time and points
you to the option that fits, from ways to pay the bill (like a payment arrangement with Dominion) to
programs that help pay it (PIPP, EnergyShare, LIHEAP, local help), with the exact phone number or link.

Program rules, dollar amounts, deadlines, phone numbers, and links come only from our sourced
decision tree. If the tree does not cover something, it points you to 2-1-1. If you mention a
disconnection notice, a shutoff, no power, or a medical device that needs electricity, it drops
everything else and follows the urgent path. It never asks for a Social Security number, account
number, or income documents. It never leaves you without a next step.

More questions come later, once this one works.
