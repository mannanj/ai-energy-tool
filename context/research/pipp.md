## 1. Current Eligibility

### Income Limit

| Criterion | Current (as of fall 2026) | Starting Jan 1, 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Income ceiling | **150% of Federal Poverty Level (FPL)** | **200% of FPL** |

- **Current law:** "Your household income is at or below 150% of the federal poverty level."  
  Source: https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ (fetched 2026-09-26).

- **2027 change:** "Beginning January 1, 2027, any retail electric customer of a Phase I or Phase II Utility with a household income at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty guidelines shall be eligible for participation in PIPP."  
  Source: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title56/chapter23/section56-585.6/ — § 56-585.6(C) (Effective January 1, 2027) (fetched 2026-09-26).

### Utility / Account Requirements

- Must be a **Dominion Energy** or **Appalachian Power (APCo/AEP)** customer.
- Must have an **active electric account** in the applicant's name (or the applicant must be responsible for the bill).
- Source: https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ (fetched 2026-09-26).

### Residency

- Must be a **resident of the locality** in which application is made.
- Source: PIPP Information Sheet PDF, page 1 — "Eligibility criteria for assistance include: Must be resident of the locality in which application is made."

### Other Conditions

The paper application (see §4) asks about:
- Citizenship / immigration status for all household members.
- Disability status.
- Current living situation (own, rent, subsidized housing, Section 8, homeless/emergency shelter, etc.).
- Whether anyone in the household receives SNAP benefits.
- The applicant must certify truthfulness, agree to report changes in 5 days, authorize DSS to obtain verification, and authorize energy supplier to share account data.

Source: https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/PIPP_Application_English.pdf (fetched 2026-09-26).

## 2. How the Benefit Works

### Percent-of-Income Caps (Monthly Bill Cap)

| Heating Source | % of Gross Monthly Income | Applies To |
|---|---|---|
| Electric primary heat | **10%** | Monthly "PIPP Amount" the customer pays |
| Non-electric heat | **6%** | Monthly "PIPP Amount" the customer pays |

Source: https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ and PIPP Information Sheet PDF — "The monthly bill amount, or 'PIPP Amount,' for a customer will be based on a percentage of the household's income and primary heat source."

**Upcoming change (effective Jan 1, 2027):** Caps drop to **5%** (electric heat) and **3%** (non-electric heat).  
Source: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title56/chapter23/section56-585.6/ — § 56-585.6(A) (Effective January 1, 2027) — "no more than three percent of the eligible participant's annual household income if the household's heating source is anything other than electricity, and to no more than five percent of an eligible participant's annual household income on electricity costs if the household's primary heating source is electricity" (fetched 2026-09-26).

### Arrearage Forgiveness Mechanics

- Each month the customer pays the **PIPP Amount in full and on time**:
  1. A credit equal to the difference between the **current bill amount** and the **PIPP Amount due** is applied.
  2. A **1/12th credit of the past-due balance** (calculated at enrollment) is applied to the account.
  3. After **12 consecutive months of on-time, in-full payments**, the entire past-due balance is forgiven.
- The customer also avoids new utility debt during the 12-month period.

Source: PIPP Information Sheet PDF — "A credit for the difference between the current bill amount and the PIPP Amount due. A 1/12th credit of their past due balance (calculated at enrollment) is applied to the account."

### Free Home Energy Audit

- "May include a free home energy audit to lower future costs."  
  Source: https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ (fetched 2026-09-26).

## 4. Documents Required

From https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ (fetched 2026-09-26):

1. **Proof of income for the last 30 days** — for all adults (over 18) in the household. The paper application specifies "Attach proof of income for the full month prior to application" and asks the income frequency (weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly).
2. **Current electric bill** (showing account name, account number, electric company).
3. **Social Security numbers** for all household members.
4. Any additional documents requested by the Local Department of Social Services (LDSS).

The paper application also requires the applicant to:
- List all sources of income (earned, self-employment, unemployment, workers comp, SSI, Social Security, veterans benefit, retirement, TANF, child support, etc.)
- Provide citizenship/immigration status for non-citizen household members.
- Provide SSN for each household member.
- Sign a certification agreeing to report changes within 5 days and authorizing DSS to verify information with the energy supplier.

Source (paper application): https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/PIPP_Application_English.pdf (fetched 2026-09-26).

## 5. Staying Enrolled / Recertification

From https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ (fetched 2026-09-26):

To remain enrolled, the customer must:
- **Re-verify income each year** (annual recertification).
- **Report changes in income or household size** — the paper application requires notification "within 5 days of any changes that occur in my situation."
- **Stay current on monthly payments** — pay the PIPP Amount in full and on time each month.

Consequences of non-compliance:
- "Missing payments, making partial payments or failing to re-verify may cause removal" from the program.
- If removed, arrearage forgiveness stops and past-due balance reverts.

Source: https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ — "Staying in the Program" section (fetched 2026-09-26).

The paper application form includes checkboxes for "Intake □ Change □ Renewal", indicating the same form is used for initial application, changes, and annual renewal.

## 6. What Changed in 2025-2026

### A. Legislation: 2026 Va. Acts ch. 690 — Major PIPP Expansion

- **Approved:** April 13, 2026
- **Effective:** January 1, 2027
- **Bill summary:** "Amends the Percentage of Income Payment Program (PIPP), which provides electric bill payment assistance to eligible customers, to include participation by any retail electric customer of Dominion Energy Virginia or Appalachian Power with a household income at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty guidelines." Also reduces caps to 3% (non-electric heat) and 5% (electric heat).
- Source: https://lis.blob.core.windows.net/files/1214569.PDF — Department of Planning and Budget bill summary (fetched 2026-09-26).
- Codified at: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title56/chapter23/section56-585.6/ — § 56-585.6 (Effective January 1, 2027) (fetched 2026-09-26).
- Full text: https://lis.blob.core.windows.net/files/1222142.PDF (fetched 2026-09-26).

Key changes taking effect Jan 1, 2027:

| Aspect | Current (150% FPL) | Starting Jan 2027 (200% FPL) |
|---|---|---|
| Income limit | 150% FPL | 200% FPL |
| Cap — electric heat | 10% of income | 5% of income |
| Cap — non-electric heat | 6% of income | 3% of income |
| Annual program cost cap | $125M (current practice) | $125M (statutory, unchanged) |

- DSS must update its rules and guidelines by **January 1, 2027** to reflect the new eligibility requirements.
- Source: 2026 Va. Acts ch. 690, enactment clause 2.

### B. SCC Case: Dominion's 2026 PIPP Update (PUR-2026-00057)

- **Filed:** May 1, 2026 by Dominion Energy Virginia.
- **Requested:** Universal Service Fee (USF) of **$0.000158 per kWh**, effective November 1, 2026 through October 31, 2027.
- **Revenue requirement:** $14,811,509 (Virginia jurisdictional).
- **Customer impact:** ~$0.16/month increase for a residential customer using 1,000 kWh.
- **Hearing/comment deadline:** July 15, 2026. Order requested by October 15, 2026.
- **Administrative cost reimbursement:** $1,581,194 (April 2025 – March 2026) from the PIPP Treasury Fund.
- Source: SCC filing at https://www.scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch/DOCS/8bz901!.PDF (fetched 2026-09-26).
- Public notice: https://fredericksburg.com/ads/community/announcements/legal/ad_fda6017e-7e5c-5b06-8fd4-b0a37c3d00f6.html (fetched 2026-09-26).

### C. 2025 PIPP Update (PUR-2025-00075) — USF of $0.00/kWh

- The 2025 PIPP Update (approved by SCC Order on October 2, 2025) set the USF at **$0.00 per kWh** for November 1, 2025 – October 31, 2026 because previously collected funds were sufficient.
- Source: SCC filing at https://www.scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch/DOCS/85dt01!.PDF (fetched 2026-09-26).

### D. Dominion-DSS Contract (March 27, 2025)

- Dominion Energy and VDSS executed a contract on March 27, 2025 outlining each entity's responsibilities for PIPP.
- This contract created processes for monthly data-sharing and reimbursement.
- Dominion began issuing past-due subsidies (accruing since February 2024) in May 2025, with almost all past-due subsidies reimbursed by June 2025.
- Source: SCC filing at https://www.scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch/DOCS/8bz901!.PDF, paragraphs 17-18 (fetched 2026-09-26).

### E. State Budget (Item 334)

- **FY 2026-2027 biennial budget:** $119.5 million per year from nongeneral funds for PIPP.
- **Annual program cost cap:** $125.0 million total, including a maximum of **$5.5 million for program administration** for state agencies and local DSS.
- Source: https://budget.lis.virginia.gov/item/2026/2/HB30/Enrolled/1/334/ (fetched 2026-09-26).
