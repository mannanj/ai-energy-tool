## 1. Program Components

Virginia's Energy Assistance Program (EAP) is the state's LIHEAP program, administered by the Virginia Department of Social Services (VDSS). It has four components. The main public-facing page is at **https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/ea/** (fetched 2026-09-26).

### Fuel Assistance (main LIHEAP component)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **What it pays for** | Offsets home heating costs for households responsible for their heating bills. A one-time payment applied to the primary heat utility account or fuel vendor. |
| **Application window (2026–2027)** | **2nd Tuesday in October through 2nd Friday in November.** In 2026: **October 13, 2026 through November 13, 2026.** (Calculated: 2nd Tuesday of Oct 2026 = Oct 13; 2nd Friday of Nov 2026 = Nov 13.) If Nov 13 is a holiday, apps accepted next business day. Eligibility notices mailed in late December. |
| **Who qualifies** | Gross monthly income ≤ 150% of the federal poverty level (FPL). Must have a heating expense (pay for home heating directly). Must reside in the locality where application is filed. |

**Sources:**
- https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/ea/ — "Fuel Assistance – Offsets heating fuel costs"; "Application window: Oct (2nd Tuesday) – Nov (2nd Friday)"; "Eligibility notices sent late December"; "To qualify… have a gross monthly income at or below 150% of the federal poverty level" (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/403-errors/FUEL-ASSISTANCE-APPLICATION.pdf — "Applications are accepted the 2nd Tuesday in October through the 2nd Friday in November" (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/eap/Complete_Manual.pdf — "The application period for the Fuel Assistance component of the EAP is the second Tuesday in October until the second Friday in November"; "eligibility notices sent late December" (fetched 2026-09-26)

### Crisis Assistance

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **What it pays for** | Heating emergencies: repair/replacement of heating equipment, supplemental heating equipment, primary heat utility bills (past-due), security deposits for primary heat, emergency fuel delivery, emergency shelter (limited). |
| **Application window (2026–2027)** | **Equipment/security deposits:** November 1 through March 15. **Fuel/utility bill payments:** First business day in January through March 15. (Fairfax County lists March 16 as close date.) |
| **Who qualifies** | Must have a heating emergency (no heat, disconnection notice, unsafe equipment, past-due amount on primary heat account). Income ≤ 150% FPL. |

**Sources:**
- https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/ea/ — "Crisis Assistance – Heating Emergencies"; "Application window: Nov 1 – Mar 15 (equipment/security deposits); Jan – Mar 15 (fuel/utility bills)"; "must have a heating emergency (no heat, cut-off notice, unsafe equipment)" (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/familyservices/public-assistance-employment-services/energy-assistance — "November 1 through March 16 for heating equipment repair/purchase/maintenance and a one-time only heat security deposit"; "The first business day in January through March 16 for purchase of home heating fuel and payment of a heat utility bill." (fetched 2026-09-26)

### Cooling Assistance

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **What it pays for** | Purchase/installation of one window air conditioner (if no working AC in home), repair of central AC or heat pump, purchase/installation of ceiling/attic/whole-house fan, payment of electric bill for cooling, electric deposits. *Note: Cannot receive a window AC if any working AC already exists in the home.* |
| **Application window (2026)** | **June 15 through August 15** (VDSS policy manual). Fairfax County lists **June 15 – August 17, 2026** for 2026. |
| **Who qualifies** | Household must pay cooling costs AND include a vulnerable person: age 60+, living with a disability, or a child under age 6. Income ≤ 150% FPL. |

**Sources:**

## 2. Income Limits by Household Size

The general rule (VDSS public site): **Gross monthly income at or below 150% of the federal poverty level (FPL).**

The 2026 HHS Poverty Guidelines (48 contiguous states) at 150% FPL produce these limits:

| Household Size | Monthly Income Limit (150% FPL) | Annual Income Limit |
|---------------|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | $1,995 | $23,940 |
| 2 | $2,705 | $32,460 |
| 3 | $3,415 | $40,980 |
| 4 | $4,125 | $49,500 |
| 5 | $4,835 | $58,020 |
| 6 | $5,545 | $66,540 |
| 7 | $6,255 | $75,060 |
| 8 | $6,965 | $83,580 |
| Each additional | +$710 | +$8,520 |

**NOTE:** The VDSS Complete Manual policy document contains a slightly different income table (e.g., HH size 1 = $1,956, size 2 = $2,643, size 3 = $3,331, size 4 = $4,018). These numbers may reflect a prior program year's FPL or a different rounding methodology. The state's public-facing page consistently says "150% of the federal poverty level." The 150% FPL table above uses the official HHS 2026 poverty guidelines and is the most current screening reference. **Always let the local DSS office make the final eligibility determination.**

**Households where all members receive SSI are automatically eligible** regardless of the income table (per the Complete Manual).

**Sources:**
- https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/ea/ — "Have a gross monthly income at or below 150% of the federal poverty level" (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/eap/Complete_Manual.pdf — Full income table: $1,956 (size 1) through $15,018 (size 20) (fetched 2026-09-26)
- HHS 2026 Poverty Guidelines — 150% multiplier calculated from the published 100% FPL figures for the 48 contiguous states

## 4. Documents Required

The exact document requirements vary by component and applicant circumstances, but the core requirements (per the Fuel Assistance application form and PIPP documents page) are:

1. **Social Security numbers** for all household members (required for members 18+ not receiving public assistance/SSI unless they hold Qualified Alien status)
2. **Current heating/electric bill** (or gas bill) showing account name and number — attach a copy to the application
3. **Income documentation** for all household members, typically:
   - **Earned income:** Pay stubs, pay envelopes, or written employer statements
   - **Self-employment:** Profit/loss documentation
   - **Unearned income (SSI, Social Security, VA benefits, etc.):** Award letters, benefit checks, or SDX/BENDEX system data
   - **Unemployment/Worker's Comp:** Award letters
4. **Heating vendor information** — name, address, account number of the primary heat fuel provider
5. **Application form** — signed by the applicant or authorized representative

The VDSS policy manual notes that an applicant's **self-declaration** is accepted for: residence, age, SSN, citizenship, Medicare premium, heat-in-rent status, and living arrangement.

The LDSS may request additional verification with a 10-calendar-day deadline.

**Sources:**
- https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/pipp/ — "Required Documents: Proof of income for the last 30 days for all adults (over 18 years old) in the household; Current electric bill; Social Security numbers for all household members; Any additional documents requested by LDSS" (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/403-errors/FUEL-ASSISTANCE-APPLICATION.pdf — Lists all fields: SSNs for all members, employer/income info, utility account info, current bill attachment requirement (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.dss.virginia.gov/media/vdss/benefit-programs/documents/eap/Complete_Manual.pdf — "Earned income is verified by pay stubs, pay envelopes, or written statements from employers"; SSN requirements; applicant statement accepted for residence, age, etc. (fetched 2026-09-26)

## 5. Stacking Rules

### PIPP + Energy Assistance (EAP)

**YES — a household can receive both PIPP and Energy Assistance simultaneously.**

The Virginia SCC order approving PIPP states explicitly:

> *"Benefits received through the PIPP will not be counted as income in determining Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), Energy Assistance Program (EAP), public assistance, or Medicaid eligibility."*

And:

> *"Households who participate in the Energy Assistance Program can be eligible for the PIPP."*

PIPP sets electric bills at 10% of income (electric heat) or 6% of income (other heat), forgives past-due balances with on-time payments, and runs year-round. EAP provides one-time seasonal payments. The programs serve different purposes and are designed to work together.

**Source:** https://www.scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch/DOCS/7yr001!.PDF — SCC order language (fetched 2026-09-26)

### EnergyShare (Dominion Energy) + Energy Assistance (EAP)

**EnergyShare is described as a "last resort" program** that is "not income based" and serves "anyone facing a crisis and in danger of losing their primary heating or cooling source." It is designed to fill gaps after state and federal assistance.

Dominion Energy's brochure states EnergyShare can be layered with other assistance. However, specific stacking rules are determined by the local screening agencies. EnergyShare application is through **2-1-1 (211virginia.org)** for a referral to a local EnergyShare agency, not through VDSS/CommonHelp.

**VERDICT:** EnergyShare appears stackable with EAP based on its "last resort" design, but this researcher could not find an explicit "you can receive both" statement on a primary source page within the search limit. **Recommend verifying with the EnergyShare agency directly** (call 2-1-1 or visit https://211virginia.org).

**Sources:**
- https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/content/paying-my-bill/energyshare/pdfs/virginia/energyshare-va-brochure-english.pdf — "EnergyShare is a last resort, year-round energy assistance program"; "not income based"; application via 2-1-1 (fetched 2026-09-26)
- https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/pdfs/virginia/billing/billing-options-and-assistance/vaenergyassistance.pdf — Overview of all programs including EnergyShare as separate from state assistance (fetched 2026-09-26)
