## 1. Eligibility Conditions — As Dominion States Them

### General eligibility (from dominionenergy.com EnergyShare page)

**Source:** https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Paying-My-Bill/Billing-Options/EnergyShare

- **Must be a resident in Dominion Energy's service area.**
- **Must have a disconnection notice, need services restored, or need help with a payment plan.**
- **Must demonstrate a financial hardship.**
- **No income restrictions for bill payment assistance recipients.** Dominion states explicitly: *"EnergyShare is not income based so any customer facing financial hardship is eligible to apply."*
- It is a **"program of last resort"** — meaning applicants should have exhausted other options first.
- Applications are reviewed **case-by-case** by partner agencies.
- If approved, the benefit is paid **directly to the energy provider** (not the customer).

### Age 60+ extra assistance

*"Individuals 60 and older may be eligible for additional assistance."* Same source as above.

**From the brochure (PDF):** Customers under 60 must have a disconnect notice or need to restore services. Customers 60+ **do not need a disconnect notice** — this is confirmed by the 211virginia.org agency listings as well.

Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/content/paying-my-bill/energyshare/pdfs/virginia/energyshare-va-brochure-english.pdf

### Veterans and persons with disabilities — extra $500

*"Military veterans and those individuals living with disabilities may qualify for an additional $500 benefit if participating in the voucher programs of the Virginia Department of Veteran Services or the Virginia Department of Aging and Rehabilitative Services' Centers for Independent Living."*

Same source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Paying-My-Bill/Billing-Options/EnergyShare

From the brochure (summary table):
| Group | Application Period | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **General** | Heating: Oct 1 – May 31<br>Cooling: June 1 – Sep 30 | Last resort program; no income guidelines; household in crisis; customers under 60 must have disconnect notice; live in Dominion service territory |
| **Veterans** | Jan 1 – Dec 31 (year-round) | A veteran; receive a housing voucher or experiencing a housing crisis; live in Dominion service territory |
| **Persons with Disabilities** | Jan 1 – Dec 31 (year-round) | Receive SSA or VA Disability Income; live in household; already receive services from CIL; live in Dominion service territory |

### Summary of eligibility requirements (exact from Dominion's brochure)

1. Must live in Dominion Energy's Virginia service territory.
2. Must be facing a crisis / financial hardship.
3. Must be in danger of losing primary heating or cooling source.
4. Customers under 60 must have a disconnect notice or need to restore service (age 60+ exempt from this).
5. No income limits.
6. It is last-resort assistance.

## 5. Dominion Energy Payment Options That Are NOT EnergyShare

### A. Payment Plans (Short-Term & Long-Term)

**Short-Term Payment Extension (Extend Your Due Date):**
- Provides more time to pay your electric bill.
- Available by signing into your online account or calling 866-366-4357.
- Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/content/paying-my-bill/energy-assistance/pdfs/virginia/energy-assistance-va-brochure-english.pdf

**Long-Term Payment Plan:**
- Splits your past due balance into **equal monthly payments (up to 6 months)**.
- Each month you pay one installment **plus your current bill**.
- A **down payment is required** to start the plan.
- A **1.5% late payment charge** is applied to the remaining past due balance on each bill until the plan is complete.
- Missing a scheduled payment may cancel the plan.
- Available for residential and small business customers.
- To set up: sign in online or call **866-366-4357** (+1-866-366-4357).
- Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Paying-My-Bill/Billing-Options (Billing Options page)

### B. Budget Billing

- **Free** program.
- Averages your previous 12 months' bills into **one predictable monthly payment**.
- Normally recalculated every 12 months.
- Eligibility: must be a customer at the same location for **12+ months** and have **no current balance**.
- If less than 12 months, call 866-366-4357 for eligibility determination.
- Late payment charge of 1.5% may apply to balances 25+ days after billing.
- **Not eligible** for payment arrangements while on Budget Billing.
- To enroll: sign in online or call **866-366-4357**.
- Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Paying-My-Bill/Billing-Options/Budget-Billing

### C. Percentage of Income Payment Program (PIPP)

- For income-challenged households at or below **150% of the Federal Poverty Level**.
- Monthly bill capped at a percentage of income: **Electric heat = 10% of income; Other heat source = 6% of income**.
- On-time, in-full payments earn incentive credits that reduce the balance.
- No deposits or late payment charges for PIPP participants.
- Applications managed by **Virginia Department of Social Services (VDSS)**.
- Apply online at **commonhelp.virginia.gov** or in person at local DSS office.
- Not eligible for other billing programs (Budget Billing, payment plans, etc.) while on PIPP.
- Must re-verify income every 12 months.
- Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/content/paying-my-bill/energy-assistance/pdfs/virginia/pipp-va-brochure-english.pdf and the Energy Assistance page.

### D. Third Party Notification

- Allows you to designate a relative, friend, or other trusted party to be **notified before service is cut off** for non-payment.
- Source: Energy Assistance brochure PDF (same as above).

### E. Medical Conditions Page — **VERIFIED**

**URL:** https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Start-Stop-Service/Medical-Conditions

**What it does:** Dominion Energy maintains a Medical Conditions list for customers who rely on electrically-powered medical equipment (respirators, oxygen concentrators, dialysis machines, etc.). Being on this list provides **priority notification in the event of a planned outage** — it does NOT automatically prevent disconnection for non-payment.

**Key details:**
- Requires a **physician's statement and signature** on the Medical Equipment Request Form / Serious Medical Condition Certification Form.
- Must be renewed **every year** (form must be received within 30 days of the anniversary date).
- Send completed forms to: **demedical@domenergy.com**
- Source: https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Start-Stop-Service/Medical-Conditions (fetched 2026-09-26)

**Important note:** Virginia SCC rules and Dominion's Terms & Conditions (Section XVI) do restrict disconnections during extreme temperatures (≤32°F or ≥92°F forecast within 24 hours) and on Fridays, weekends, state holidays, or the business day prior to a state holiday. A serious medical condition may qualify for additional protections under SCC rules, but the specific Medical Conditions page on dominionenergy.com primarily addresses outage notification rather than disconnection delay. For disconnection protection related to serious illness, clients should call **1-866-DOM-HELP (866-366-4357)** and ask about medical-certificate disconnection protections.

Source for temperature/weather disconnection rules: https://www.dominionenergy.com/-/media/content/rates-and-tariffs/pdfs/virginia/terms-conditions/vatc16ra.pdf

Source for Medical Conditions page: https://www.dominionenergy.com/en/Virginia/Start-Stop-Service/Medical-Conditions
