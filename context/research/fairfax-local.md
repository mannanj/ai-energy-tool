## 2. Fairfax County DFS Energy Assistance Programs

Fairfax County Department of Family Services (DFS) runs several energy assistance programs. The main DFS phone is **(703) 324-7500**, TTY 711.

### Programs

#### A. Fuel Assistance (Heating)
- **What it covers:** Helps eligible low-income households pay heating costs
- **Open application period:** Second Tuesday in October through second Friday in November **(mark your calendar — next window opens ~Oct 13, 2026)**
- **How paid:** Approved applicants get benefit in late December; payments run Jan–Apr or until exhausted

#### B. Crisis Assistance
- **What it covers:** Households with no heat or imminent danger of losing heat
- **Eligible expenses:** Payment of heating utility bills, security deposits, heating fuel purchase, heating equipment repair/replacement (homeowners only for replacement)
- **Open application period:** Nov 1 – Mar 15 (heating season); heating equipment repair/purchase/maintenance and one-time heat security deposit available Nov 1 – Mar 15; heating fuel purchase and utility bill payment starts first business day in January – Mar 15

#### C. Cooling Assistance
- **What it covers:** Payment of electric bills for cooling equipment, security deposits, AC repair, fan purchase/installation, AC unit purchase/installation
- **Eligibility:** At least one household member must be 60+, ≤5 years old, or disabled (per Social Security, Medicaid, or 100% VA)
- **Open application period:** June 15 – August 17 **(closed for 2026)**. Opens again June 2027.

#### D. Percentage of Income Payment Program (PIPP)
- **What it is:** Dominion Energy customers with income ≤150% of federal poverty level can cap monthly bill at a % of gross income
- **Electric primary heat:** 10% of gross monthly household income
- **Non-electric primary heat:** 6% of gross monthly household income
- **Cannot apply by phone —** apply online at CommonHelp.Virginia.gov
- **Year-round program**

## 6. Why Bills Went Up — Dominion Energy Rate Changes (2025–2026)

### Summary for a Plain-Language Chat Helper

> **"Your electric bill went up because Dominion Energy got approval from the state to raise its rates for the first time in over 30 years. Here's what happened and why."**

### The SCC Biennial Review (Case PUR-2025-00058) — BASE RATE INCREASE

| Item | Dominion Requested | SCC Approved |
|------|-------------------|--------------|
| 2026 revenue increase | $822 million | **$565.7 million** (cut by 31%) |
| 2027 additional increase | $345 million | **$209.9 million** (cut by 39%) |
| **Typical residential monthly impact — 2026** | $14.73 | **$11.24** (7.5% increase) |
| **Typical residential monthly impact — 2027** | $4.84 | **$2.36** (1.5% increase) |

- **Effective dates:** January 1, 2026 (2026 increase) and January 1, 2027 (2027 increase)
- **Basic monthly customer charge:** Increased from **$7.58 to $9.58** effective January 1, 2026
- **First base rate increase since 1992**

**Why, according to the SCC and Dominion:**
1. **First base rate increase in decades** — Dominion's base rates had not been significantly increased since 1992
2. **Rising operational costs** — labor, maintenance, materials (same inflationary pressures affecting everything)
3. **State-mandated projects** — infrastructure upgrades and environmental compliance
4. **Expired fuel credit** — a prior credit that had been reducing costs expired
5. **New power generation** — adding more power sources to meet demand

**What the SCC said in its Final Order:**
*"The concern associated with rate increases generally, and at this juncture in particular, is understandable. However, many of the same inflationary, economic, and policy impacts that have affected other segments of the economy providing goods and services to consumers apply as well to companies providing electric service."*

The SCC also **rejected** Dominion's proposal for a new data center customer class with reduced minimum charges — the Commission said it was not adequately supported.

### Fuel Factor Adjustment (Case PUR-2025-00059)

- Dominion also filed for a fuel factor revision
- Combined base rate + fuel factor request was larger than the base alone
- The SCC's Final Order notes that the fuel factor hearing was combined with the base rate hearing

### RGGI (Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative) — COMING March 2027

**Virginia rejoined RGGI effective July 1, 2026**, and Dominion filed to reinstate Rider RGGI (Case PUR-2026-00076):

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Estimated monthly impact** | **$13.00/month** for typical residential customer (1,000 kWh) — effective March 1, 2027 |
| **Mitigation proposal** | Dominion proposed spreading costs over 2 years → lowers impact to **$10.36/month** |
| **BUT — RGGI credits required** | Virginia law now requires RGGI allowance proceeds be returned to residential/small business customers as a **line-item credit** on bills. The actual net impact may be **lower** than $10.36–$13.00 once credits are factored in. |
| **When it takes effect** | March 1, 2027 (rate year: Mar 2027 – Feb 2028) |

**Plain language on RGGI:** *"Virginia re-entered a regional program that makes power plants pay for carbon emissions. Dominion will pass those costs to customers starting March 2027 — roughly $10–$13/month more. However, the state also requires Dominion to give customers a credit from the auction proceeds, which will partly offset the increase."*

### Total Picture — What a Typical 22310 Resident Is Seeing (Fall 2026)

| Component | Amount | When |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Base rate increase (2026) | +$11.24/month | Jan 1, 2026 |
| Basic monthly charge increase | +$2.00/month | Jan 1, 2026 |
| Base rate increase (2027) | +$2.36/month | Jan 1, 2027 |
| RGGI rider (estimated) | +$10.36–13.00/month | Mar 1, 2027 (less credits) |
| Fuel factor changes | Varies | Ongoing adjustments |

**Key message for the chat helper:** If a resident's bill went up in the past year, the main driver is the $11.24/month base rate increase from Jan 2026 (largest single increase). If they're worried about next year, the 2027 increase ($2.36) and RGGI ($10–13, partially offset by credits) are coming.
