Budget Billing
Plan for your bill with our free Budget Billing program.

Budget Billing FAQs
What is Budget Billing?

Budget Billing is a free, convenient budgeting tool to help you avoid seasonal swings in your monthly bill. It keeps your payment consistent month after month, reducing surprise spikes and allowing you to plan for the payment you’ll owe.

Who can enroll in Budget Billing?

Budget Billing is available to residential customers, small and mid-size business customers, and places of worship. You must have at least 12 months of billing history at your current address and must not owe more than your current bill to enroll. If you have a previous balance, it must be paid in full before you can enroll in Budget Billing.

How is the payment calculated?

Your Budget Billing amount is based on the average of your past 12 months of bills.

Are there any additional fees or charges when I enroll in Budget Billing?

There are no fees to enroll in Budget Billing. Other charges, such as HomeServe, will be separate from your Budget Billing amount each month.

Are my actual charges shown on my bill?

Yes. Your bill will show your actual energy usage and charges, your total amount due and your Budget Billing payment amount. You simply pay your Budget Billing payment amount each month.

Will my Budget Bill amount change during the year?

Your amount could change. Normally, your payment amount is recalculated every 12 months to reflect your actual usage. If your energy use is much higher or lower than usual, it may affect your amount at your annual review. Usage Alerts can help you stay aware of higher-than-usual energy use, so you can make adjustments along the way.

Why did my Budget Billing amount go up so much from the previous year?

Your Budget Billing payment amount is recalculated every year to reflect your actual average energy use and any regulated rate changes or service additions that impact your area. Some of these costs impact all Dominion Energy customers regardless of participation in Budget Billing. It’s a good idea to review your usage regularly to help keep your energy use in line with last year’s levels. Usage Alerts can make that easier by letting you know when your energy use is higher than usual, so you can make adjustments along the way.

What happens if I pay more or less than I owe over the course of the Budget Billing plan period?

If your payments are more than your actual charges, we owe you. We'll apply the amount owed to your recalculated Budget Billing payment amount for the next 12 months. If the total of your payments is not enough to cover your actual charges for the previous year, you owe us. You can either pay the difference in full or have the difference added to your recalculated Budget Billing payment amount for the next 12 months.

What if I miss a payment?

Budget Billing payments must be made in full by the due date to avoid being removed from the Budget Billing program. A late payment charge of 1.5% may be added to any balance remaining 25 days after billing.

If I move, will I still be on Budget Billing?

If you move to a new address while on Budget Billing, your account will be unenrolled, and your entire balance will be due. You cannot reenroll in Budget Billing until you have established 12 months of usage at your new address.

If you need more time to pay off your account balance, please give us a call at 866-366-4357.

Can I pay my actual account balance instead of the Budget Billing amount?

While on Budget Billing, you should only pay the budget amount (not your current charges or the actual account balance).

Are payment arrangements allowed on the Budget Billing plan?

No, if you are on Budget Billing, you are not eligible for payment arrangements.

To establish an arrangement, you will need to first stop Budget Billing. Sign in to your account to see if you qualify for a payment arrangement.

How do I unenroll from Budget Billing?

You can stop Budget Billing at any time by signing in to your account or on the Dominion Energy app.

At the time you cancel, your actual account balance will be due. Any credit balance will be applied to your next bill.
