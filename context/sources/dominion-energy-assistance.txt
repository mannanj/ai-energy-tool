Set Up a Payment Plan

A long-term payment plan lets you:

Split your past due balance into equal monthly payments (up to 6 months).
Each month, you‘ll pay one of those payments plus your current bill.

Important to know:

A down payment is required to start the plan.
A 1.5% late payment charge will be applied to the remaining past due balance on each bill until the payment plan is complete.
Paying extra goes toward your next payment.
Missing a scheduled payment may cancel your payment plan.
Plans are available for both residential and small business customers.

To see if you qualify and set up a payment plan:

Sign in to your account or
Call us at 866-366-4357.

Percentage of Income Payment Program (PIPP)

PIPP helps eligible customers afford their electric bill. The program can lower monthly bills, reduce past-due balances, and offer free home energy audits. Applications are handled by Virginia Department of Social Services.

State Fuel Assistance helps pay electric bills used to heat your home.

To be eligible, you must:

Be responsible for your heating expense.

State Crisis Assistance

State Crisis Assistance helps during heating emergencies.

Am I eligible?

To be eligible, you must:

Have a heating emergency, such as:
Have no heat.
Received a shut-off notice.
Have unsafe heating equipment.

EnergyShare® Heating

EnergyShare® helps pay heating bills for customers facing financial hardship.

Am I eligible?

To be eligible, you must:

Be facing a crisis.
Be responsible for your heating expense.

Note: There are no income restrictions.

When can I apply?
October 1 – May 31
How much?
$600 maximum

State Cooling Assistance

State Cooling Assistance helps pay energy bills during hot summer months.

Am I eligible?

To be eligible, you must:

Be responsible for your cooling expense.
Be living with a child 5 or younger, a senior age 60 or older, or someone who is certified as disabled.
Meet income requirements.

Other Resources
2-1-1 Virginia

This free service can refer you to other utility bill help programs in your area, from non-profits to houses of faith.

All referrals are free and confidential.
2-1-1 Virginia is available 24/7.
What kind of help?

Trained professionals can refer you to:

Utility and rental assistance programs
Food banks
Shelters
Physical and mental health resources
Childcare and elder care
And much more
Important to know:

2-1-1 Virginia doesn't give financial help or take applications for any programs. Instead, 211 Virginia can connect you to available programs in your area.
