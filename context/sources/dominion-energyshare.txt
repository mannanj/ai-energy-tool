EnergyShare®
Financial Assistance for Those in Need

EnergyShare® is an energy assistance program of last resort that helps people who are having trouble paying their energy bills. Help is available year-round, regardless of income. The program can also provide free home energy upgrades to help you use less energy.

View Transcript
How Does It Work
EnergyShare® can help in two ways:
Helping Pay My Bill

EnergyShare® helps with energy bills for any type of heating or cooling (oil, natural gas, kerosene, propane, wood, and electricity).

You may be eligible if you:

Live in Dominion Energy's service area.
Have received a disconnection notice.
Need your service reconnected.
Need help keeping your payment plan.
Have received a Budget Billing removal notice.

Local agencies look at applications and decide who is eligible. If approved, the payment is sent directly to your energy company.

Help is available all year:
	Heating	Cooling
Dates	Oct. 1 – May 31	June 1 – Sep. 30
Benefit Amount	Up to $600	Up to $300
If you're 60 years of age and older, you may qualify for help more often.
Military veterans and people with disabilities may qualify for an extra $500. To qualify you must be enrolled in an approved voucher program through one of these organizations:
Virginia Department of Veterans Services
Virginia Department of Aging and Rehabilitative Services' Centers for Independent Living
Apply

To apply, you can find your local EnergyShare® agency by calling 2-1-1 or visiting 211virginia.org. You can also view this list for your agency's contact information.

Visit 211virginia.org 
Free Home Energy Upgrades

You may also be able to get a free home energy assessment. A contractor will visit your home and recommend weatherization tips, and ways to help you use less energy and lower your energy costs.

Upgrades are based on your checkup and may include:

LED light bulbs
Low-flow showerheads and faucet aerators
Attic/floor insulation
Heat pump or air conditioner tune-ups
Air and duct sealing
Hot water tank and pipe wrap insulation

You may qualify if you:

Are a Dominion Energy customer.
Have received bill payment help.
Apply

To apply, call 888-366-8280 and select the Income and Age Qualifying option. You will be connected with a contractor in your area.

How to Donate

EnergyShare® is supported by Dominion Energy and the donations of employees, customers, and community partners. You can help support local families by making a donation using one of the options below.

Donation Portal

Visit our secure online portal to make a donation.

If you are a Dominion Energy customer, sign in to your account. Select "Billing" at the top of the page, and then "Donate to EnergyShare®." Then select "Make a Donation." Your donation will appear on your next bill.
