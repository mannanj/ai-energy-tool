   How It Works
        Pay your reduced bill in full and on time each month
        Each month, 1/12 of your old balance is forgiven
        After 12 months of on-time payments, your past-due balance is gone

   Staying in the Program
   To remain enrolled, you must:
        Re-verify your income each year
        Report changes in income or household size
        Stay current on monthly payments
   Missing payments, making partial payments or failing to re-verify may cause
   removal.

   How to Apply
   Option 1: Apply online through CommonHelp
   Option 2: Submit a paper application to your Local Department of Social
   Services (LDSS)
