           Cooling Assistance

          Cooling Assistance is a program designed to help keep vulnerable Fairfax County residents coo
          during the summer months. To be eligible, at least one household member must be: 60 or older,
          younger, or disabled according to Social Security, Medicaid or 100% Veteran’s Administration.

          The open application period for Cooling Assistance is from June 15 - August 17, 2026.

          Cooling assistance can help with:

               Payment of electric bills to operate cooling equipment.
    Payment of security deposits for electricity to operate cooling equipment for accounts in da
    of being disconnected or when a deposit is needed to begin service.
    Repair of a central air conditioning system or heat pump.
    Purchase of a whole-house fan, including ceiling or attic fans when there is no other working
    cooling equipment in the home.
    Purchase and installation, or self-pick-up, of one air conditioning unit (window or portable)
    households when there is no other working cooling equipment in the home.

Additional Cooling Resources

    Senior Cool Care Program: If you’re 60 or older, you may also be eligible for a fan or air
    conditioning unit through the Senior Cool Care Program. To learn more, please call the Agin
    Disability & Caregiver Resources Line at 703-324-7948.
    AC Rescue: If you are not eligible for Cooling Assistance, you may be eligible for small air
    conditioning (AC) equipment through the AC Rescue program, operated by Fairfax County's
    Office of Environmental and Energy Coordination (OEEC). Learn more


Fuel Assistance

The Fuel Assistance Program helps eligible households in meeting their energy needs by
supplementing the cost associated with their primary heat source. Eligible households must me
income requirements.

Open application period for the Fuel Assistance Program is the second Tuesday in October thro
the second Friday in November.

Approved applicants will be notified of their benefit amount in late December. Payments begin i
January and continue through April or until the benefit has been exhausted.

Crisis Assistance

The Crisis Assistance Program is designed to help households overcome energy emergencies th
cannot be met by other resources. An energy emergency is determined when a household has n
heat or is in imminent danger of being without heat. Crisis Assistance offers assistance with one
more of the following:

    Payment of primary heat source utility bills.
    Payment of security deposits for primary heat utility.
    Purchase of primary home heating fuel when the fuel supply is low.
    Repair of inoperable or unsafe heating equipment.
    Replacement of heating equipment for homeowners only (when repair is not possible).
    Provision of supplemental heating equipment/maintenance.

Open application period:

    November 1 through March 16 for heating equipment repair/ purchase/maintenance and a
    one-time only heat security deposit.
    The first business day in January through March 16 for purchase of home heating fuel and
    payment of a heat utility bill.

Percentage of Income Payment Program (PIPP)

PIPP helps eligible Dominion Energy customers maintain their electric service by making utility b
more affordable.

The monthly bill amount, or “PIPP Amount,” for a customer will be based on a percentage of the
household’s income and primary heat source.

    10% of gross monthly household income when the household has electricity as their primary
    heat source or
    6% of gross monthly household income when the household does not have electricity as the
    primary heat source.

Each month a customer makes the required PIPP payment on-time and in-full, the customer:

    Avoids new utility debt
    Reduces old utility debt

A portion of the customer’s actual account balance is removed through incentive credits applie
the account.
