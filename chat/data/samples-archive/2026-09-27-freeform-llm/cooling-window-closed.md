First, a quick question: **Do you have a disconnection notice, is your power already off, or does anyone in your home use medical equipment that needs electricity?**

Resident: No disconnection notice or anything like that. No medical equipment either, just a normal household.

Helper: Good, glad it's not urgent.

Let me ask a few questions to see what programs you might qualify for. First: **Who sends your electric bill – Dominion Energy or NOVEC?**

Resident: It’s Dominion, yeah. They send it every month.

Helper: Great. Next question: **How many people live in your home?**

Helper: Thanks. One more: **Roughly what is everyone's total income per month, before taxes?**

Resident: It’s around $3,100 a month, before taxes. My husband works and I stay home with the little one.

Helper: With three people, the income limit for most help programs is about $3,331 per month. You're under that, so you may qualify for several options.

Since you're asking about cooling costs specifically, here's what's available:

**Cooling Assistance** (federal LIHEAP money) – This helps with electric bills for cooling equipment, AC repair or purchase, and fans. But it's closed for the year – it runs from June 15 to August 17. It opens again June 15, 2027.

**Fuel Assistance** – Even though it's called "fuel," it supplements your primary heat source. If you heat with electricity, this can help with electric costs. It opens **October 13, 2026** and runs through November 13. Apply at commonhelp.virginia.gov or call 833-522-5582.

**PIPP (Percentage of Income Payment Program)** – This is a year-round program. If you heat with electricity, your monthly electric payment becomes 10% of your income. For you that would be about $310 a month. If you heat with gas or oil, it's 6% (about $186). Each on-time payment also wipes out 1/12 of any past-due balance. You apply at commonhelp.virginia.gov.

**Do you heat your home with electricity, or with gas/oil/something else?** That decides which PIPP percentage applies.

Resident: We heat with gas, so it’d be the 6% one. That might actually help us out a bit.

Helper: That's a good option. With gas heat, your PIPP payment would be about **$186 a month** – and each on-time payment erases 1/12 of any past-due balance you have.

**Are you behind on the bill at all? Roughly how much?**

Resident: Not behind at all, we’ve been keeping up so far. Just the summer bill was a lot to swallow.

Helper: That's good you're current. PIPP still works for you – it sets your monthly payment at 6% of income going forward. But since you're not behind, you won't get the past-due credit benefit right away.

Here's your next step:

**Apply for PIPP at commonhelp.virginia.gov.** You'll need:
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for all adults over 18
- Your current electric bill
- Social Security numbers for everyone in the household

If you want help with the application, call **Fairfax DFS at 703-324-7500** (Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.) or walk into the **Alexandria Human Services Center at 8350 Richmond Highway, Alexandria, VA 22309**.

Also, since you're current and just want to lower costs, you could look into **Budget Billing** from Dominion – it gives you the same payment every month based on your average usage. Call **866-366-4357** or sign in at dominionenergy.com. You need 12 months of billing history at your address and must not owe more than your current bill.

Want me to go over either of those in more detail?
