# Can I just pay the same amount every month so I can plan?

Resident: Can I just pay the same amount every month so I can plan?

Helper: Yes, you can. That is called **Budget Billing**.

Here is how it works:
- Dominion looks at your last 12 months of bills and averages them.
- You pay that same amount every month.
- It is recalculated once a year. If you overpay, the difference is credited to next year's amount.

**A few rules:**
- You need 12 months of billing history at your current address.
- You must not owe more than your current bill. Any past-due balance must be paid in full first.
- There are no fees.

If you are behind right now, you cannot start Budget Billing yet. You would need a payment plan first to catch up, then switch to Budget Billing later.

To set it up, sign in at **dominionenergy.com** or call **866-366-4357**.

Would you like to check whether you qualify for a payment plan to get caught up first?

Resident: oh i dont owe anything past due right now, so i think i can do this. is there any catch with the yearly true up? like if i used more would i get a big bill?

Helper: Good question. Here is how the yearly true-up works:

- Each year, Dominion recalculates your average. If you used more electricity than your monthly payment covered, the difference is added to next year's monthly amount — not a lump sum.
- If you used less, the difference is credited to next year's payments.

So you will not get a surprise big bill at the end of the year. The adjustment just changes what you pay each month going forward.

One thing to know: if you move, Budget Billing ends and your full balance comes due at once. So if you think you might move soon, keep that in mind.

Want to go ahead and sign up? You can do it online at **dominionenergy.com** or call **866-366-4357**.
