# When can I apply for heating help this year and how?

Resident: When can I apply for heating help this year and how?

Helper: Fuel Assistance opens **October 13, 2026** and closes **November 13, 2026**.

You can apply:

- **Online:** commonhelp.virginia.gov
- **By phone:** 833-522-5582 (833-5-CALL-VA)
- **By mail:** 12011 Government Center Parkway, Suite 232, Fairfax, VA 22035
- **By fax:** 703-653-1355
- **By email:** send a photo of the completed form to DFSMycase@fairfaxcounty.gov
- **In person:** at any Fairfax DFS Human Services Center. The one closest to 22310 is the **Alexandria Human Services Center** at 8350 Richmond Highway, Alexandria, VA 22309. They're open Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Have your electric bill and proof of income for the last 30 days ready. The program is for households at or under 150% of the federal poverty level. Do you want me to check whether your income fits?

Resident: Oh thanks, that helps. We make about $4,500 a month for 5 of us—would we even qualify at that income?

Helper: Let me check the income table for a household of 5.

The limit for 5 people is **$4,706 per month**. Your income of $4,500 is under that, so yes, you would qualify.

You should apply when Fuel Assistance opens on **October 13**. Apply online at commonhelp.virginia.gov or by phone at 833-522-5582.

Do you heat your home with electricity, or with gas or something else? That matters for another program called PIPP that could really lower your monthly bill.

Resident: Oh, we use electric heat, it’s all electric. That’s good to know about the limit—so we can do it. For PIPP, is that a separate application or do we ask about it when we call?

Helper: PIPP is a separate application. You cannot apply for it by phone.

You apply online at **commonhelp.virginia.gov** — the same site as Fuel Assistance. You can apply for both at the same time.

Here's what PIPP does for you: since you heat with electricity, your monthly electric payment would become **10% of your gross household income**. For you, that's about **$450 per month** instead of your full bill. Each on-time month, part of your past-due balance gets wiped away. After 12 months of on-time payments, the past-due balance is gone.

You will need:
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for all adults over 18
- Your current electric bill
- Social Security numbers for everyone in the home

Do you have those ready to apply?

Resident: Yeah, I can get those together—pay stubs and the bill are pretty easy. So I just do the online app for both on commonhelp, and that’s it?

Helper: That's it. Apply online at **commonhelp.virginia.gov** for both Fuel Assistance and PIPP at the same time.

A couple of things to keep in mind:

- **Fuel Assistance** opens **October 13** — that's about two and a half weeks from now. Apply as early as you can.
- **PIPP** is year-round, so you can apply anytime. But until your enrollment is complete, you still owe the normal bill amount.
- If you fall behind before PIPP kicks in, call **Dominion at 866-366-4357** and ask for a payment plan to buy yourself time.

Also, since you're all electric, you might want to check into free home energy upgrades through Dominion's EnergyShare program — things like insulation, air sealing, and LED bulbs. That's a separate call to **888-366-8280**.
