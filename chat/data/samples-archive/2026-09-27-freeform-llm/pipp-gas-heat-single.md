# Is there any program that makes my electric bill lower every month? I'm on a fixed income.

Resident: Is there any program that makes my electric bill lower every month? I'm on a fixed income.

Helper: Yes, there is a program that can lower your monthly electric bill based on what you earn. It's called **PIPP** (Percentage of Income Payment Program).

Here is how it works:

- If you heat your home with electricity, you pay **10%** of your gross monthly household income.
- If you heat with gas or oil, you pay **6%**.
- The minimum payment is $10 per month.

For example, if your household income is $1,000 a month and you heat with electricity, your PIPP payment would be $100 per month.

**To qualify, you need all three:**
1. Be a Dominion Energy customer.
2. Have a household income at or below 150% of the federal poverty level.
3. Have an active electric account.

Let me check if you might qualify. About how many people live in your home, and roughly what is everyone's total income per month before taxes?

Resident: Just me in the house. My income is around $1,700 a month from Social Security. That's before they take out Medicare part B.

Helper: Thank you. Based on what you've told me:

- **Household size:** 1 person
- **Income:** $1,700 per month

The income limit for one person to qualify for PIPP is **$1,956 per month** (from the state's table). So you are under that limit — you likely qualify.

**Do you heat your home with electricity, or with gas or oil?** That decides whether your PIPP payment would be 10% or 6% of your income.

Resident: It's gas heat. So that'd be the 6% then, right? That comes to like $102 a month, which sounds real good compared to what I pay now.

Helper: Yes, that's right. If you heat with gas, your PIPP payment would be **6% of $1,700**, which is **$102 per month**. That is a fixed amount based on your income, no matter how much electricity you use.

There are a few things to know:

- **Until PIPP is approved**, you still owe your normal bill each month.
- **Each on-time payment** wipes out 1/12 of whatever you were behind when you enrolled. After 12 months of on-time payments, the past-due balance is gone.
- You will need to **re-verify your income each year** and report any changes.

**To apply, you have two options:**

1. **Online:** commonhelp.virginia.gov
2. **Paper application** through Fairfax County Department of Family Services (DFS).

You cannot apply by phone. If you want help with the form, call **703-324-7500** (Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.) or walk into the **Alexandria Human Services Center** at 8350 Richmond Highway, Alexandria, VA 22309.

**Documents you will need (do you have these handy?):**
- Proof of income for the last 30 days (your Social Security award letter works)
- Your current electric bill
- Social Security numbers for everyone in the home (just you)
