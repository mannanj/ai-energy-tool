# Why

Rates are going up. People have questions about their electric bill: why it went up, how to pay it,
and whether any help exists. Help does exist, but it is scattered across agencies and websites, and
people don't know which option fits them, whether they qualify, or who to call.

Many feel ashamed to ask a person. A chat window has no judgment and is open at 11pm. It also helps
the pastor, social worker, or family member who is trying to help someone and can't memorize every
program.
